How the bill is calculated

The bill has three parts. The drink set is charged once per room. TC is charged for each guest. The room fee is charged once per room. Other venues in Gangnam use the same structure, and two guests usually pay ₩410,000–510,000.

Ask for the total, not the drink price

Some ads show only the drink set price. TC and the room fee are then added at the end. Tell us how many guests and what time you arrive, and ask for the total. Keep the message so you can check it when you pay.
