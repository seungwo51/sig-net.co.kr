Summary

  • Seoul KTV venues are concentrated in Gangnam, mainly Sinsa, Nonhyeon, Yeoksam and Daechi.
  • From Myeongdong or Itaewon it takes about 20–25 minutes by subway to Sinsa Station. From Gangnam Station it is three stops.
  • The subway stops around midnight. After that you go back by taxi, so keep your hotel name in Korean.

Visitors staying in Myeongdong or Hongdae often look for a KTV near their hotel and find only singing rooms. The room-type KTVs with a drink set are south of the river, in Gangnam. The trip is short if you know the route.

Getting back late at night

Seoul subway lines stop running around midnight. Most guests leave after that, so plan on a taxi.

  • Save your hotel name and address in Korean before you go out.
  • Use a taxi app, or ask staff to call a taxi for you.
  • Fares are higher late at night. Keep some cash or a card that works in Korea.

FAQ

Q. Are there KTVs in Myeongdong, Hongdae or Itaewon?

Those areas mostly have singing rooms (noraebang) and bars. Room-type KTVs with a drink set are concentrated in Gangnam.

Q. Which Seoul KTV is closest to a subway station?

Signet is 2 minutes on foot from Sinsa Station Exit 3.

Q. How much is a KTV in Seoul?

Two guests pay ₩410,000–510,000 in Gangnam. At Signet it is ₩460,000, or ₩410,000 before 9 pm.

Q. Can you book a venue near my dinner place?
