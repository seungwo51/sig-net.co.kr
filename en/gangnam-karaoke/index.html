Summary

  • Gangnam karaoke can mean a noraebang, where you pay by the hour to sing, or a room karaoke (KTV) with drinks and a hostess.

Three kinds of karaoke in Gangnam

This site covers the third kind. If you only want to sing, any noraebang near Gangnam Station will do and you do not need a booking.

How a night at a Gangnam karaoke goes

  1. ArriveMessage us from the hotel entrance. We meet you and take you down.
  2. Take a roomYou are shown a room that fits your group. Signet has 42 rooms.
  3. The set is servedWhisky, mixers and snacks come to the table and the karaoke machine is turned on.
  4. Extend or finishBefore the basic time ends, staff ask if you want to stay. Extra time adds TC of ₩140,000 per guest.
  5. PayCheck the bill against the total you were sent, then pay.

FAQ

Q. Is a Gangnam karaoke the same as a noraebang?

No. A noraebang is a room you rent by the hour to sing. A Gangnam room karaoke includes a whisky set and a hostess, and costs ₩460,000 for two at Signet.

Q. Does the karaoke machine have English, Japanese and Chinese songs?

Korean karaoke machines carry foreign songs, but the list differs by venue. Ask when you book if you need a specific language.

Q. Can we order food?

Yes. Signet has 36 food and snack items and 20 coffee and tea items. Food is charged separately.

Q. Do we have to drink whisky?

The basic set is whisky. Ask about other drinks when you book.

In short

If you want to sing, go to a noraebang. If you want the Gangnam night out, book a room karaoke and get the total first.
